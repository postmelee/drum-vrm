# Work-record instructions

Follow [root instructions](../AGENTS.md) and [document ownership](../specs/policies/documentation.md). Read the issue-specific plan rather than the whole archive.

- Preserve framework-managed manuals, templates and skills apart from reviewed project placeholder substitutions.
- Put adopted contracts in specs, proposals/research in tech, stage evidence in working, final/historical evidence in report, and owner decisions in feedback.
- Use English for new records. Preserve original Korean historical evidence.
- Record the actual tested commit, method, environment, result and limitation. Never turn prior test counts into current guarantees.
- Keep indexes short. Do not paste current issue/PR status or duplicate measurements across plans and guides.
