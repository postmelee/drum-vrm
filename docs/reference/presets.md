Standard, deep and compact are individual part-shape choices. The procedural kit styles are variants of project geometry, not three separately acquired model assets. Original external binaries are preserved; runtime adapters identify parts, normalize coordinates and fit playing surfaces. The development shared-hardware path displays bodies with application-owned stands and pedals, rather than reproducing every source component.

Exact creator links, acquired file hashes, license evidence and adaptations belong to the provenance records above and [asset notices](../../public/models/NOTICE.txt). [Original license texts](../../public/models/README.md) remain alongside the files. CC BY attribution and source conditions apply to those assets; MIT does not replace them.

Arbitrary local GLB bodies need preparation and visual inspection. Follow [replace drum parts](../how-to/replace-drum-parts.md). The application has one crash role; extra source cymbals do not create independently mapped crash notes.

Externally modeled electronic kits and other surveyed candidates are research possibilities, not installed presets. Their original audit is preserved in the [asset research record](../../mydocs/tech/research/2026-10-24/drum-assets-followup.md).
